The year is 2053

Humanity's last hope

Twenty years ago, an incident brought humanity to the brink of destruction. Today, the truth is still buried — and the mysterious Enemys are back.

As a pilot for the shady organization C.O.R.E., you are the world's last line of defense. Fly against impossible odds, uncover what really happened two decades ago, and do everything you can to prevent another catastrophe.

No place and no time is safe: from neon Mega-Cities to the open ocean, abandoned military bases and beyond the atmosphere.

Your mission

  • Defend Earth from the Enemys
  • Uncover the incident of 20 years ago
  • Survive combat on land, at sea, in the air and in space

The developer

About Gernot Lepschy

Project CORE is an independently developed passion project by Gernot Lepschy — programmer, level designer and writer. Built with Unity and Blender, it aims to bring a unique and fresh experience to both experienced gamers and newcomers.

From level design and programming to story and visuals, Project CORE is crafted by one developer — supported by the G.L. Studios name.
